RoundTimer Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 8, 2026

Summary

RoundTimer does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Your privacy is fully protected.

Data Collection

RoundTimer collects no data whatsoever. Specifically:

  • No personal information is collected
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs are included
  • No network requests are made — the app works entirely offline
  • No user accounts or sign-in is required
  • No advertising frameworks are included

Data Storage

All app data (your timer presets, workout history, and settings) is stored locally on your device only. This data is never transmitted to any server.

Third-Party Services

RoundTimer does not use any third-party services, analytics, or advertising platforms.

Children's Privacy

RoundTimer does not collect any data from any user, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever change this policy, we will update this page. Given that RoundTimer collects no data, we do not anticipate any changes.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at shtark285@gmail.com.
